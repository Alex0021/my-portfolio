A lightweight personal budget tracker built as a quick "vibe coding" experiment --- no framework, just HTML, CSS, and vanilla JS to scratch a practical itch.

---

## What I Built

A simple web app to track income and expenses, with category breakdowns and visual summaries. The goal was speed over polish: get something functional fast, learn by doing, and see how far raw web fundamentals go without a framework.

---

## Why It Matters to Me

Not every project needs to be complex. This was about scratching a real need quickly and enjoying the process of building something from nothing in a single sitting.

I’m a naturally curious and hands-on engineer who enjoys understanding how complex systems work as a whole. Learning is part of my everyday routine — whether it’s exploring new tools, debugging tricky problems, or experimenting with side projects. People around me often describe me as organized, reliable, and open-minded, and I try to bring that mindset into both teamwork and independent work. I also value balance: helping others, staying active, and keeping a healthy rhythm are just as important as technical growth.

A graduate of Polytechnique Montréal in electrical engineering, I had the opportunity to explore a field that particularly intrigued me, the energy sector, through the IGEE program. Wanting to continue growing and take on new challenges, I chose to pursue a master’s degree abroad, in Lausanne, Switzerland. This experience allows me to combine two of my major interests: deepening my knowledge of robotics at one of the most recognized technological institutions in Europe, while also exploring and discovering a new cultural environment.

Robotics represents a particularly motivating field for me, as it brings together several of my areas of interest — electronics, programming, and control — in a context that is both concrete and innovative. It also embodies an opportunity to contribute to technological solutions with real-world impact, with the long-term goal of delivering tangible value to society.
